Found by the v0.1.0 review: on a body with an unclosed code fence, `update --handoff` exits 0 but `show` returns `handoff: null`, and a second `--handoff` adds a second section (`DES-HANDOFF`, `REQ-HANDOFF`).

- [ ] After building the new body, require that reading the handoff back gives the note just written; otherwise fail with `invalid_handoff` and write nothing
- [ ] A `VER-HANDOFF` test through the binary covers the unclosed-fence body
