Found by the architecture audit after milestone zb-v90b0k, at e1c9354. Structural debt.

`ErrorCode` (`src/error.rs`) and the three fixed vocabularies (`src/issue.rs`, about lines 14–63) generate an `ALL` list from one definition, so the guide and the tests cannot miss a new value. Two sets do not:

- `NotReady` (`src/relations.rs`, about lines 36–62): `src/guide.rs` (about lines 300–311) and `tests/prime.rs` (about lines 334–347) each list its six forms by hand, with a dummy ID replaced by `<id>`. Adding a reason, as zb-banzx5 did with `ancestor_draft`, compiles without updating either list.
- `ProblemCode` (`src/issue.rs`, about lines 142–224): `tests/check.rs` (about lines 18–32) hard-codes the 13 codes of `DES-CHECK`, and `Warning.code` in `src/operations.rs` (about lines 99–105 and 496–513) uses string literals where `ProblemCode` already serialises to the same text.

Relevant items: `DES-READINESS`, `DES-CHECK`, `DES-AGENT-GUIDE`.
